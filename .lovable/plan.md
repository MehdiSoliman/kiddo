

# 📚 Ma Journée d'École - School Day Diary

A gentle, kid-friendly app to help your daughter remember and share her school day activities with you.

---

## 🎨 Design Vision

**Soft & Calm Aesthetic**
- Pastel color palette (soft lavender, mint green, peachy pink, sky blue)
- Rounded, friendly shapes throughout
- Gentle illustrations and playful but calm icons
- Large, touch-friendly buttons perfect for tablet use
- Clean layout that doesn't overwhelm

---

## 🏠 Main Features

### 1. Today's Timeline
The heart of the app - a beautiful visual timeline showing the 5 key moments of the school day:

- **🌅 Début d'école** (Start of school)
- **☕ Récréation du matin** (Morning break)
- **🍽️ Déjeuner** (Lunch)
- **🎈 Récréation de l'après-midi** (Afternoon break)
- **🏠 Retour à la maison** (Back home)

Each time block is a tappable card that opens to add activities.

### 2. Adding Activities
When you tap a time block, you can:
- **Pick from visual icons** - Common activities like:
  - 📝 Writing/Écriture
  - 📖 Reading/Lecture
  - 🔢 Math/Mathématiques
  - 🎨 Art/Dessin
  - 🎵 Music/Musique
  - 🤸 Sports/Gym
  - 🌳 Outside play
  - 🧩 Games/Puzzles
  - 💬 Talking with friends
  - And more!

- **Type custom activities** - For anything not in the icons (you type, it saves with a custom emoji)

- **Special for lunch** - A text field to type the menu

### 3. Calendar Diary
- Browse past days with a simple calendar view
- Tap any date to see what she did that day
- Perfect for "Remember last week when you..." conversations
- Visual indicator showing which days have entries

### 4. Today at a Glance
- Summary view of today's filled timeline
- Quick way to review the day together before saving

---

## 💾 How It Works

- All data saved locally on your device (browser storage)
- No login or account required
- Entries are automatically saved as you add them
- Works offline once loaded

---

## 📱 Experience

- Optimized for tablet/computer with spacious layout
- Also works on phone if needed
- French-friendly (we can add French labels!)
- Designed for parent-child interaction - you ask, she points, you both enjoy
